Editing-project drafts are faster and need almost no spare disk space. Each page's transparency is read in every frame as the page is drawn. An opaque page is written straight to H.264 (or ProRes 422 HQ), with no lossless copy first and no second pass. A page with any transparent pixel is drawn again at once in the same browser tab, as lossless PNG (or ProRes 4444), exactly as before. A draft of a music video whose pages were held as gigabytes of PNG while it was made now needs no more room than the draft itself.
