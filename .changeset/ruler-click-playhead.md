Desktop: a click on the timeline's ruler moves the playhead and a drag scrubs, as in other editors. To refer a moment to the chat, ⌘-click the ruler (Ctrl-click on Windows), the key that draws a box on the picture; ⌘-drag refers a span. While the key is held the cursor turns into a crosshair and the moment under it is drawn.
