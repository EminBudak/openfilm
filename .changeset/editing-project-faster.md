Export › Editing project is two to four times faster, with the same files. A 剪映 or CapCut draft's pages are drawn as a quick lossless PNG and written once at the end (H.264, ProRes, or PNG as before): ffmpeg's own PNG settings took more processor time than drawing the page. Pages of every editing project are drawn several at once with the browsers the export already uses, each page's check and re-encode running while the next ones draw. On an M5 Mac a 50-second film of ten full-frame pages went from 153–190 s to about 50 s, and a 95-second music video from 334 s to 145 s.
