Export: pick the exact frames and ranges to export, in a larger dialog that grows with the window.

- Images and Slides & PDF are one page, Images, with four ways to deliver the same frames and framing: image files, a ZIP, a PDF or a PowerPoint deck. Any frame can be picked, not only the middle of each shot. The film shows large, as it will be exported, over a filmstrip that zooms and scrolls like the timeline. Click or drag to look, then Add this frame or Return; ← → step a frame. Quick picks add the playhead, the middle of each shot, each marker, or a frame every few seconds. Each image is named after the shot it is in, and the picks are remembered for each project.
- A partial video, GIF, audio or subtitle export can take up to 100 ranges. Add a range, trim it on the filmstrip or type its start and end (seconds, M:SS, H:MM:SS or a frame timecode), and play it in the preview. Export each range as its own file, or merge them into one file in the order listed, with picture, sound and subtitles kept together. A range that is not valid stops the export with a message; the whole film is never exported in its place.
- Video file has the social media presets: Default first, then each platform. Changing a setting makes it Custom. Saved social export settings carry over.
