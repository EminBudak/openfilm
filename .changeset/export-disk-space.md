Exports need far less free disk space while they run. A page's frames were on the disk three times while its file was made; now they are there twice at most, and a page drawn by one browser is moved into place instead of copied. This matters most for editing projects, whose pages are drawn as lossless PNG first, so a long page is gigabytes. An export that fills the disk now says so plainly, in your language, instead of showing ffmpeg's log.
