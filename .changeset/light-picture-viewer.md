A picture opened in the chat opens in a light viewer: the window dims, the picture shows at its own size in the middle (at most nine tenths of the window), and Esc, a click beside it or × closes it. Download is in its right-click menu. The full-window frame with its name, size, Fit / 100% and Download is gone for pictures; video, sound and documents open as before. An Esc pressed just after one that closed a viewer no longer stops the running turn.
