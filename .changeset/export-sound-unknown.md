Exports keep their sound more reliably. "Include sound" is on each time the export opens: switched off once, it used to stay off, and every later video came out silent without anyone noticing. A film counts as silent only once every video in it has been checked, so the Audio export no longer says a film has no sound while its videos are still being read. A file made again at the same path is checked again for sound, instead of being remembered as silent until Studio restarts.
