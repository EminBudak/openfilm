Export › Editing project: a choice of how the pages are written. A 剪映 or CapCut draft is automatic by default: each page is drawn with its transparency, as Studio shows it, and every frame is read; a page with no clear pixel in any frame comes as H.264 (the graphics chip's when there is one), and one with any transparency as lossless PNG, each picture kept once for as long as it shows. A one-minute film of full-frame pages went from 2.5 GB to under 20 MB. All PNG, or ProRes (422 HQ for an opaque page, 4444 for one with transparency), can be picked instead. Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve keep ProRes 4444 by default, with PNG to pick; Final Cut Pro takes ProRes 4444.
