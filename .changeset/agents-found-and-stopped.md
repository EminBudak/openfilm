Agents installed with nvm, fnm, mise, Volta, asdf, Bun or pnpm are found even when the login shell does not answer, and on Windows those installed under fnm. Closing a chat stops the agent's own program and its MCP servers too, not only its adapter. On Windows, a `%` in a word given to a `.cmd` agent stays as it is.
